Learn Kanji: Pen-Enabled privacy policy

Last updated: 2 November 2026

Learn Kanji: Pen-Enabled is a kanji writing practice app that runs in your browser. It does not collect, store or share personal information.

  • Your writing, progress and settings are stored only in your browser's storage on your device.
  • The app has no accounts, analytics, advertising or tracking, and its lessons work without an internet connection once it is loaded.
  • Basic 1 needs no purchase. If you buy Learn Kanji Full (a subscription or a one-time purchase), Gumroad handles the payment, any renewals and your receipt; Gumroad's privacy policy applies to that: gumroad.com/privacy. The app never receives your payment details.
  • When you enter a license key, the app sends the key to Gumroad's license service to check that it is valid, and checks it again now and then while you are online. Only the key and the product it belongs to are sent. Gumroad's answer includes the purchase's details; the app keeps only the plan and its dates, on your device.
  • When you tap a speaker button, your device's built-in speech feature reads the word aloud. Some voices provided by the browser or operating system may use an online service; the app does not control or receive that data.
  • Backups you save are files on your own device. The app does not upload them.
  • Invite and thank-you codes for friends are made on your device from a random number. They contain no personal information, and the app never sends them anywhere: you share them yourself if you want to.

Clearing the site's data in your browser, or removing the installed app, removes everything the app stored.
